Opens the session summary overlay — the same thing `/summary` shows. Press again, or Esc, to close.

The overlay holds the rolling summary ayin keeps once a conversation gets long enough that older turns compress out of the agent's live context — it's the fastest way to check what survived that compression before trusting a follow-up to earlier detail. Shares its slot with the tool-output overlay (Ctrl+O) — opening one closes the other. PgUp/PgDn scroll it while it's open.
