Turns on full explanations. Without it, ayin answers as tersely as the question allows.

Terse is the default because most turns are read while you're waiting for a build or a test — a paragraph of reasoning where one line would do is a paragraph you skim past. Turn verbose on before something you'll actually want to read carefully: a design decision, a tradeoff, a "why did you do it this way." It takes effect on your next message, not retroactively.

## Examples

    /verbose
    /verbose off
