Arduino project{{GREENFIELD_NOTE}}.

Build-breaking rules:
- Sketch filename = its folder name, character for character. RgbCycle/RgbCycle.ino builds;
  RgbCycle/main.ino and servo-dial/servo_dial.ino do not. Hyphens are legal in both — never
  "normalise" a hyphen to an underscore in one name and not the other. Rename both or neither.
- analogWrite works only on PWM pins. Uno/Nano: 3, 5, 6, 9, 10, 11. Any other pin is on/off only.
- Every LED, and each colour channel of an RGB LED, needs its own series resistor.
- INPUT_PULLUP reads LOW when pressed, no external resistor. Wired to 5V instead it needs a
  pull-down or the pin floats. State which this plan uses.

Component facts — pinouts, leg order, polarity, whether a part needs a resistor — come from the
catalog below, not from recall. Call arduino_db(id=…) for a full entry (every leg, what it connects
to, wiring notes) on anything you actually wire. Use web_search only for what the catalog lacks: a
datasheet, a library's API, a board variant's pin capabilities.

After the sketch is written, call the arduino_diagram tool. It reads the sketch's real pins and this
catalog and writes <sketch>.wiring.puml plus a rendered .svg. Never write a .wiring.puml yourself: a
hand-drawn one is ungrounded, and a plausible wrong pinout gets wired.
