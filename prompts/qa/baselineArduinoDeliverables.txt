Three things exist on disk when an Arduino project is finished. The evidence states, as measured fact, whether each does — trust that over any claim in the agent's message. (1) SKETCH: a .ino inside a folder of the same name. (2) README at the project root: what the circuit does, the parts list, the pin map, the arduino-cli compile and upload commands. Headings with nothing under them are a placeholder, not a README. (3) WIRING DIAGRAM: <sketch>.wiring.puml beside the sketch, plus its rendered .svg. Fail on any evidence line reading MISSING, naming the exact path to create. If all three are present, do not invent a fourth.
