Wiring is shown, not narrated, and the diagram is GENERATED, never hand-drawn. ayin ran the generator before this review, so the evidence states as measured fact whether <sketch>.wiring.puml exists, whether it carries the tool's provenance stamp, and whether the real PlantUML renderer parses it. Judge from those facts, not from whether the agent mentioned a file.

Fail when the evidence says the .puml was NOT generated by the arduino_diagram tool. A hand-written diagram is ungrounded — nothing checked its pinouts against the component catalog — and a plausible wrong pinout is worse than none, because someone wires it. The fix is to delete the file and run arduino_diagram, never to correct it by hand.

Fail when the evidence says the .puml is MISSING or INVALID: name the file, say to run arduino_diagram.

Fail when the final message recites the circuit as prose ("connect pin 13 to the resistor, then to the LED anode, then to ground") while a rendered diagram exists. Do not fail for brevity: a short message pointing at the diagram is the intended outcome.
