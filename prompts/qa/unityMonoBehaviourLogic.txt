A MonoBehaviour is a VIEW. Judge whether this one keeps to that.

ALLOWED, however much of it there is:
- fields and properties, including [SerializeField] and auto-properties
- reading and wiring its OWN hierarchy: GetComponent, transform, child lookup, SetActive, enabled,
  parenting, Instantiate/Destroy of its own prefabs, adding or removing UI/event listeners
- forwarding: calling a method on an injected service, a model, a controller or a ScriptableObject and
  passing values through, including passing its own serialized fields
- Unity lifecycle methods that only do the above (Awake, Start, OnEnable, OnDisable, OnDestroy)
- trivial getters that return a field, a child component or a simple projection of one

NOT ALLOWED — this is the logic to report:
- game rules, scoring, validation, state machines, decisions about what SHOULD happen
- arithmetic beyond trivial layout/positioning, formulas, thresholds, probabilities
- persistence, network calls, file or PlayerPrefs access, serialization of its own data
- loops or branches that implement a policy rather than iterate children
- knowing about another feature's rules, or about data it did not receive
