Look at an image and judge it yourself.

The image is queued for your next turn — the reply you get back is a receipt, not the picture. Say what
you expect to see BEFORE you look; an expectation written afterwards is not evidence.

Use it whenever what you changed shows up as something rendered:
  matplotlib / seaborn   save the figure to PNG, then look at it
  sphinx / LaTeX         build, then `pdftoppm -png -r 100 -f 1 -l 1 out.pdf /tmp/page`, then look
  pyreverse / graphviz   render to PNG, then look
  a web page             screenshot it, then look

Raster only (png, jpeg, webp, gif). Convert first, then look at the conversion.
