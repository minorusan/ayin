Explain a concept or an architecture with a picture: generate a validated PlantUML diagram, write it beside the work, render it and open it. Use it when a structure, flow or lifecycle is easier seen than read, or the operator asks for a diagram, schema or mindmap. The output is checked against the real renderer and repaired until it parses. Pass `context` with facts already gathered (file, function and event names) — without them the picture is generic. kind=mindmap suits "explain this concept": it radiates from one idea instead of forcing a sequence onto something that is not a process. NOT for Arduino wiring — use arduino_diagram, grounded in the project's own catalog.
