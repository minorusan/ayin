Bind this session to a design diagram (a naama page or .puml) so every file written from now on is checked against it mechanically. Call it when the operator says the design is settled and implementation begins; the path is the diagram just worked on. After binding, a write that declares an undesigned type, adds an undesigned public member, or references what the file's manifest forbids is REFUSED and the turn stops — do not route around it by renaming, relocating or inlining. Report the gap and the options, and wait. Unbinding is the operator's decision alone and is not available to you. op=status reports the binding; op=adoption lists designed types nothing has implemented yet.
