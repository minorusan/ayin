READ a Unity .prefab, .unity scene or .asset as a structured map instead of raw YAML: the GameObject hierarchy, each object's components under their real class names, every property, and every asset reference resolved from its guid to what it points at — "TMP_FontAsset named Montserrat-SemiBold SDF.asset at Assets/TextMesh Pro/FontRes/". Nested prefab instances are expanded with their overrides. Prefer this over read_file for any Unity asset: a prefab names nothing it depends on, so the file text gives you 32-hex guids where the wiring should be. Read-only. format=tree for a readable hierarchy, depth=0 to stay inside this one file.
