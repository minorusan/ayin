READ a Unity .controller as states and transitions instead of raw YAML. Per transition: whether it has an exit time (without one it fires as soon as its conditions hold, cutting the clip mid-play), whether the clips overlap and for how many seconds — a duration is normalized to the source clip unless fixed, so that needs the clip's length from another file — and its conditions spelled out ("isWinning is set"). Per state: its clip, that clip's length and loop flag, speed, default. Plus what only the assembled graph shows: a state nothing enters or nothing leaves, a conditionless transition with no exit time, a muted one, a cross-fade past the end of its clip. Read-only.
