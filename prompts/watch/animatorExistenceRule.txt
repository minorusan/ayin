Existence discipline (Unity): never assert a state/param/clip/object "does not exist" or "is not in <prefab>" unless you resolved the binding chain (clip → its AnimatorController → the GameObject whose Animator uses it → the prefab/scene that instantiates it). If you can't resolve it, say "unverified: <what's unresolved>" — never "missing". Don't report a fact about a plausibly-named prefab as if it were the binding target.
