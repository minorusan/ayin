Classify each numbered line as live debug output or not.

Live debug output is a statement whose only effect is printing or breaking for a developer:
Debug.Log/LogWarning/LogFormat, print, Console.Write*, System.Diagnostics.Debug.*, a bare
Debug.Break, a temporary stopwatch or frame-time dump that nothing reads.

NOT debug output: error and exception reporting the product relies on (Debug.LogError,
Debug.LogException, Debug.Assert), a logger the codebase uses as its logging facility, a string
passed to something other than a printer, and anything inside a comment or a disabled block.
